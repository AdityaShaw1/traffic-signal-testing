# Traffic Signal System Requirements

## Functional Requirements

### REQ-001
The system shall initialize with the Red light ON.

### REQ-002
The system shall transition from Red to Green.

### REQ-003
The system shall transition from Green to Yellow.

### REQ-004
The system shall transition from Yellow to Red.

### REQ-005
Only one traffic light shall be active at a time.

### REQ-006
The traffic signal shall continuously repeat its cycle.

### REQ-007
The system shall recover safely after power failure.

### REQ-008
The system shall support pedestrian crossing functionality.

### REQ-009
The system shall support emergency mode.

### REQ-010
Invalid timer configurations shall be rejected.
