# BUG-001

## Title
Red and Green lights turn ON simultaneously

## Severity
Critical

## Priority
P1

## Module
Traffic Signal Controller

## Environment
Traffic Signal Simulator v1.0

## Preconditions
Traffic signal is running.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Start the traffic signal.
2. Wait for Red → Green transition.
3. Observe the lights.

## Expected Result

Red should turn OFF before Green turns ON.

## Actual Result

Red and Green remain ON simultaneously for approximately 1 second.

## Impact

Conflicting traffic instructions may be displayed.

## Status

Open
